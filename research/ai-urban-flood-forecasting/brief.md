# Brief — AI-based urban flood forecasting projects (document review)

Report language: Vietnamese (Tiếng Việt). Final deliverable: one detailed comparison table, one row per project.

Research question: For each documented project that deployed AI for urban flood forecasting, what do the documents say about the following?

## Information checklist
- I1 — Phạm vi và quy mô triển khai (scope & scale: city/area, km², catchments, population, project period, partners, budget, operational vs. pilot)
- I2 — Hạ tầng mạng lưới thoát nước (drainage network: sewer length, manholes, pipes, pumping stations, retention basins, rivers/canals, network model detail)
- I3 — Mô hình AI dự báo (architecture, input data, output, lead time, resolution)
- I4 — Chỉ số đánh giá độ chính xác (metrics: NSE, RMSE, MAE, CSI, R², hit rate, ...; distinguish calibration of hydraulic model vs. training/validation/test/verification of AI)
- I5 — Nguồn dữ liệu training (hydrological/hydraulic simulations e.g. 1D/2D models, synthetic rainfall, radar, gauges, surveys, crowd data, ...)
- I6 — Kết quả mô hình dự báo (results, speed-up, performance, operational outcomes)
- I7 — Hạ tầng CNTT để huấn luyện (GPU/CPU/HPC/cloud, training time, software frameworks)
