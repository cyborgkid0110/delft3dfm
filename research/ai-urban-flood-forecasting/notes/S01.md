## I2 — Drainage network infrastructure

- **F11 | I2** — The 2D model represents the sewer network only in a simplified way, as source/sink terms or areal rainfall deduction; no explicit 1D sewer network model is described.
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.26 (printed p.20) | §2.1.3 a) "Erzeugung hydrodynamischer Trainingsdaten"
  Anchor: "vereinfachte Darstellungen des Kanalnetzes in Form von Quell- und Senktermen"
  Context: Roughness values differentiated by land use (settlement, vegetation, industry).

- **F12 | I2** — Lessons learned: accurately modelling the reduction of water volume without explicitly representing the sewer network is difficult and led to uncertainty in the forecasts. Follow-up work plans coupling with sewer-network-oriented models.
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.47 (printed p.41) | §2.2 "Herausforderungen und Lessons Learned"; PDF p.49 (printed p.43) | §2.5
  Anchor: "ohne explizite Berücksichtigung des Kanalnetzes schwierig ist"
  Context: Admitted limitation of ISRV.

- **F13 | I2** — Hydrodynamic model: MIKE+ / MIKE FM ("MIKE Flow Modell (2D)"), based on the shallow water equations, with an unstructured adaptive mesh (finer at building edges, narrow streets, complex topography). Buildings are built into the DTM ("Building-Shape-Methode"). Mesh resolution is 4 m² in urban areas to 9 m² in non-urban areas. The model covers surface runoff and its interaction with water bodies ("Gewässern"). Large areas are split into sub-units with buffers at the boundaries.
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.12 (printed p.6) | Table 1 AP1; PDF p.26–27 (printed p.20–21) | §2.1.3 a); PDF p.17 (printed p.11) | §1.4.2
  Anchor: "differenzierte Auflösung von 4 m² urban bis 9 m² nicht-urban"
  Context: Resolution is given as an area (m²), presumably cell area. The model was "um zusätzliche Module erweitert" (PDF p.18, printed p.12) without specifying which.

- **NEG | I2** — S01 does not report sewer length, numbers of manholes/pipes, pumping stations, retention basins or named rivers/canals for Aachen (searched: Kanal, Haltung, Schacht, Pump, Rückhalte, km, Gewässer, Bach). The only related items: "Rückhaltebecken" appears only as a generic prevention measure in the action timeline (PDF p.38, printed p.32), and the local network operator monitors "Kanälen" (PDF p.39, printed p.33).

## I3 — AI forecasting model

- **F14 | I3** — Flood forecast model "floodGAN" (Hofmann & Schüttrumpf 2021): a GAN translating rainfall rasters into high-resolution flood maps. Generator = U-Net encoder-decoder; discriminator = PatchGAN; losses = adversarial + L1. Outputs are flood depths and flooded areas.
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.28 (printed p.22) | §2.1.3 c) "DL-Modellentwicklung"; PDF p.13 (printed p.7) | Table 1 AP3
  Anchor: "basierend auf U-Net und Patch-GAN-Discriminator, adversarial und L1-Loss"
  Context: Elsewhere described as a "Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)-basiertes DL-Modell" (PDF p.19, printed p.13).

- **F15 | I3** — Input: rainfall forecasts (radar nowcasts of the KISTERS X-band radar) are the main input. Output: flood maps for the city of Aachen at a time step of 5 minutes over a horizon of 1 h 55 min, at a spatial resolution of 2 m, written as tagged TIF images.
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.29 (printed p.23) | §2.1.3 c) "Modellintegration"
  Anchor: "in einer zeitlichen Auflösung von 5 Minuten über einen Zeitraum von einer Stunde und 55 Minuten"
  Context: New input files automatically trigger the model. Results reach datasphere through an ETL module that checks every 15 min for new files.

- **F16 | I3** — Lead time of the rainfall nowcast (driver of flood lead time): nowcast product is 2 h ahead, 125 m, 5 min, updated every 10 min. FSS analysis: useful for 15–40 min at 100 m resolution and 25–60 min at 5 km; effective warning time about 30 min. The municipal action timeline states that the ISRV forecast model provides detailed information 30 min to 2 h before the event.
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.23 (printed p.17) | Table 3; PDF p.24 (printed p.18) | §2.1.3 a) "Analyse"; PDF p.38 (printed p.32) | Fig. 11 text
  Anchor: "eine effektive Vorwarnzeit von etwa 30 Minuten ermöglichen"
  Context: Lead time is limited by the nowcast skill, not by the AI model. §2.6 (PDF p.51) gives warning times of 15–60 min for pySTEPS nowcasting in general.

- **F17 | I3** — Training scope limitation: the trained forecast models were developed only for 1-hour scenarios, which limits the prediction of longer or more complex events.
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.47 (printed p.41) | §2.2
  Anchor: "ausschließlich für 1-Stunden-Szenarien entwickelt wurden"
  Context: Future work: extend the forecast horizon and use RNN/LSTM extensions for temporal dynamics (PDF p.49, printed p.43).

- **F18 | I3** — Rainfall scenario generator "synRainGAN" (Welten et al. 2022): a GAN trained on historical rainfall (DWD RADOLAN and KISTERS rainfall rasters). The generator maps a random vector through a fully connected layer, transposed convolution and upsampling layers to a 32 × 32 rain field (batch normalisation, ReLU, tanh). The discriminator uses convolutional layers and a final fully connected layer. Two-phase training: the discriminator is trained on half-batches of real and synthetic fields until it reaches a defined accuracy, then the generator is trained against the frozen discriminator. Adam optimiser, learning rate 0.0002.
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.25–26 (printed p.19–20) | §2.1.3 a) "Entwicklung eines Starkregenszenarien-Generators"
  Anchor: "mittels transponierter Faltungs- und Upsampling-Schichten in ein 32 × 32 Regenfeld"
  Context: This is a generator of training input, not the flood forecast model. The cell size of the 32×32 field is not given.

- **F19 | I3** — Nowcasting (non-AI): pySTEPS optical flow extrapolation; four motion methods were compared (VET, DARTS, Proesmans, constant). VET was chosen: slow, but gives the best forecasts ("Best dynamic! No edge issues. Good smoothness!"). Test implementations also used EUMETSAT H SAF rainfall estimates as input.
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.22–23 (printed p.16–17) | §2.1.3 a) "Radar-Basiertes Nowcasting", Table 2
  Anchor: "Für den Radar-Nowcast wurde die VET Methode gewählt"
  Context: DARTS "very fast", constant "fast", Proesmans "slow" (Table 2 image).

## I4 — Accuracy metrics

- **F20 | I4** — [UNCLEAR — verification of the non-AI rainfall nowcast input, not of the AI model] Fraction Skill Score (FSS) of the KISTERS radar nowcast against X-band radar estimates: 10 events in Aachen in 2022–2023 (each ≥2 h long, ≥1 mm/h observed). Reference FSS_uniform = 0.5 + f0/2 = 0.543. Mean FSS was evaluated at four scales (100 m, 1 km, 5 km, 10 km) for lead times up to 120 min (Fig. 4). Useful skill (FSS > FSS_uniform): 15–40 min at 100 m; 25–60 min at 5 km. Accuracy varies by event.
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.24–25 (printed p.18–19) | §2.1.3 a) "Analyse" | Fig. 4
  Anchor: "FSSuniform = 0.5 + fo/2 = 0.543"

The AP1 summary says the 2022 data show sufficient accuracy for lead times up to 60 min to serve as DL-model input (PDF p.18, printed p.12).

## I5 — Training data sources

- **F26 | I5** — Training data workflow: historical radar data (DWD RADOLAN) trains a GAN, which generates more than 900 synthetic high-intensity heavy-rain scenarios for Aachen. 160 heavy-rain events were simulated in MIKE (2D, 61 km²) at 5-min steps (including after-run time). Water-level maps combined with rainfall rasters form the AI training datasets.
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.11–12 (printed p.5–6) | §1.3 Table 1 AP1
  Anchor: "Simulation von 160 Starkregenereignissen in 5-Minuten-Schritten (inkl. Nachlaufzeit)"
  Context: The report does not explain how many of the >900 generated scenarios fed into the 160 simulations, or any train/validation/test split. Anchor for 900: "Generierung von über 900 synthetischen hoch-intensiven Starkregenszenarien".

- **F27 | I5** — Training data contents: the simulations produced water-level maps, flow-velocity distributions and flood extents. The GAN rainfall training used historical rainfall data, e.g. DWD RADOLAN or KISTERS rainfall rasters. Synthetic fields are claimed to be free of measurement artefacts.
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.25 (printed p.19), PDF p.27 (printed p.21) | §2.1.3 a)
  Anchor: "Trainingsdatensätze in Form von Wasserstandskarten, Fließgeschwindigkeitsverteilungen"
  Context: Training data is entirely simulated (synthetic rainfall into the hydrodynamic model). No observed flood data was used for training.

- **F28 | I5** — Operational input data: KISTERS X-band radar (at KISTERS HQ / premises in Oberforstbach near Aachen, about 8 km from Aachen) gives rainfall intensity every 5 min at 25 m and 125 m resolution. Also used: DWD station data, DWD composite radar products, Citizen Weather Observer Program data, 4traffic and RWTH weather stations (3 stations, 11 parameters), and DWD ICON-D2 (0.02° ≈ 2 km, 15 min or 1 h, 48 h ahead, updated every 3 h).
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.21 (printed p.15) | "Radardaten"; PDF p.9 (printed p.3); PDF p.16–17 (printed p.10–11) | §1.4.2; PDF p.43–44 (printed p.37–38) | Table 7

- **F29 | I5** — Mobility data (not used for flood AI training): 8 detector boxes over LoRaWAN, ASEAG API, Ac-DatEP sensor data, InfluxDB API, and FCD (floating car data, penetration typically 2–8%).
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.27–28 (printed p.21–22) | §2.1.3 b); PDF p.41 (printed p.35)

## I6 — Results

- **F30 | I6** — Speed: floodGAN delivers high-resolution flood maps "in seconds" (§2.6) or "in a few minutes" (§2.4), giving a large speed-up over classical hydrodynamic models without significant accuracy loss. No timing measurement is given.
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.51 (printed p.45) | §2.6; PDF p.49 (printed p.43) | §2.4; PDF p.28 (printed p.22) | §2.1.3 c)
  Anchor: "in Sekunden hochaufgelöste Überflutungskarten liefert"
  Context: The two statements differ (seconds vs. minutes). "Minutes" may include the end-to-end pipeline into datasphere; the report does not say. §2.4 anchor: "Überflutungskarten werden in wenigen Minuten generiert".

- **F31 | I6** — Operational demonstrator: floodGAN is coupled automatically with real-time radar nowcasts in KISTERS datasphere and was shown during the 2 May 2024 event, enabling early identification of critical hotspots. Model runs from the last 3 days stay in the system cache; older runs are archived.
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.30–31 (printed p.24–25) | §2.1.3 c) | Figs. 6–7
  Anchor: "Die Funktionsfähigkeit und Praxistauglichkeit des Demonstrators konnte exemplarisch"
  Context: Single event, qualitative.

- **F32 | I6** — Hydrodynamic model speed-up from GPU: CUDA parallel finite-volume computation gives simulations in hours to minutes, which would take several hours on CPU-based systems.
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.26–27 (printed p.20–21) | §2.1.3 a)
  Anchor: "können hochauflösende Simulationen beispielsweise wenigen Stunden bis Minuten realisiert werden"
  Context: For the physical training-data model (MIKE+), not the AI model. No hardware is specified.

- **F33 | I6** — Traffic application: flood rasters from IWW, aggregated to 5-min intervals, for intensities of 30, 40, 47 and 70 mm/h, each evaluated at 30, 60, 90 and 120 min. These define the scenarios for traffic-control concepts. Wading-depth thresholds: cars <0.3 m, public transport <0.3 m, emergency services (BOS) <0.6 m. A QGIS Python tool derives the passable road network (<0.3 m) per scenario, plus closures, signage and detours. An example concept was made for Westbahnhof (scenario 40 mm/h after 90 min). Flooding has hardly any effect on existing critical supply infrastructure in Aachen.
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.35 (printed p.29) | §2.1.3 e) "Erstellung von Verkehrsführungskonzepten"; PDF p.33–34 (printed p.27–28) | Figs. 8–10
  Anchor: "Wattiefe <0,3m"
  Context: The report does not state whether these rasters come from MIKE+ or floodGAN.

- **F34 | I6** — Municipal action timeline: DWD warnings 24–48 h ahead; ISRV forecast 30 min–2 h ahead; public transport can adapt operations about 90 min before flooding; localised warnings possible from 2 h before.
  Locator: S01 | PDF p.38–39 (printed p.32–33) | Fig. 11 and text
  Anchor: "werden 30 Min bis 2 h vor dem Ereignis durch das ISRV-Vorhersagemodell"
