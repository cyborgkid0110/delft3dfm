## I3 — AI forecasting model

- **F07 | I3** — The AI computes dynamically in real time the max. flooded area and associated water depths for the next 24 h; inputs are rainfall and discharge forecasts plus past measured values.
  Locator: S05 | PDF p.3 | §3 "Ergebnisse"
  Anchor: "die max. überflutete Fläche und zugehörige Wassertiefen in den zukünftigen 24h"
  Context: Architecture (KNN type) not specified in S05; S06 specifies ResNet18-based CNN.

- **F08 | I3** — Pump-station-specific failure scenarios defined by pumping capacity; one specifically trained AI model per scenario; additionally a tool deriving failure probabilities describing conditional dependencies between failure mechanisms (Bayesian network per S06).
  Locator: S05 | PDF p.3 | §3
  Anchor: "für jedes Szenario ein spezifisch trainiertes KI-Modell aufge-stellt"
  Context: Number of scenarios not stated in S05 (S04/S06: 8).

- **F09 | I3** — Operational forcing: based on the DWD ensemble forecast ICON-D2, flood forecasts are computed for three different rainfall/discharge developments, each shown on a map; export as PDF report via integrated function.
  Locator: S05 | PDF p.3 | §3
  Anchor: "Auf Basis der Ensemble Vorhersage ICON-D2 des Deut-schen Wetterdienstes"
  Context: S04 names it "ICON-D2-EPS" with 20 members, 10/50/90 % percentiles (S04 PDF p.7, printed p.6). S04 says the report is uploaded to LV's FTP server; S05 says export as PDF — both may apply.
