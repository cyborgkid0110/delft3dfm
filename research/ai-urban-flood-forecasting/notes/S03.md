## Metadata
- Title: "GIS Derived Real-Time Impact Analytics to Support Flood Emergency Managers" (PDF p.1; PDF metadata title "...to support Flood Emergency Managers")
- Presenter: Juliette Murphy (FloodMapp); PDF metadata author: "Juliette Murphy (Australia) and Henry Walshaw". Contact juliette@floodmapp.com (p.43).
- Event: FIG Working Week 2025 + Locate25, Brisbane, Australia, 6–10 April 2025 (p.1 banner and diagonal watermark "Presented at the FIG Working Week 2025, 6-10 April 2025 in Brisbane, Australia").
- Type: commercial slide deck, 43 slides (pdfinfo reports 43 pages; pages are A4 rotated 90°, i.e. landscape slides). No printed slide numbers; locator = PDF page = slide number.
- PDF created 17 Jun 2025, modified 1 Jul 2025.
- Nature: mostly screenshots/photos with titles; very little body text. Numbers come mainly from slides 8, 11, 26, 27, 29. Text extraction (pdftotext) was combined with rendered PNG inspection of all 43 slides.
- Important scoping note: "Brisbane" is the conference venue. The deck presents the FloodMapp DASH product generally, with event case studies in Queensland (Tropical Cyclone Alfred, March 2025, incl. Brisbane suburbs; Western QLD floods, March–April 2025), New South Wales (Transport for NSW), and the USA (Norfolk VA, Lodi NJ). Predominantly riverine/catchment flooding; no explicit urban drainage (pipe) modelling.

## Negative results
- **NEG | I2** — S03 does not report sewer/stormwater network data (no pipe lengths, manholes, pumps, retention basins, or 1D network model). Searched text + all slide images for: sewer, pipe, drain, stormwater, manhole, pump, culvert, 1D, network. Only rivers/creeks and gauges appear.
- **NEG | I3** — S03 does not name an AI architecture (searched: LSTM, CNN, neural, deep learning, random forest, surrogate, emulator, model type), nor numeric lead time (only inferred 6H/24H/72H layer names, F13), nor radar nowcasting input.
- **NEG | I4** — S03 reports no statistical skill metrics (searched: NSE, RMSE, MAE, CSI, R², hit rate, accuracy %, bias, skill). No split between hydraulic-model calibration and AI training/validation/test. Only one forecast-vs-observed peak (F15) and visual comparisons (F16). "Property level accuracy" (p.8) is an unquantified claim.
- **NEG | I5** — S03 does not state the AI training dataset (no synthetic rainfall scenarios, number of training events/simulations, training period). Data types listed only under "Calibration & Validation" (F14) and "Big Data" (F17).
- **NEG | I6** — No computational speed-up or runtime figures (beyond hourly update cycle); no quantified operational benefits.
- **NEG | I7** — S03 does not report training hardware or software frameworks (searched: GPU, CPU, HPC, cloud, AWS, Azure, TensorFlow, PyTorch, training time). Only serving stack (GeoServer, Esri ArcGIS Enterprise/cloud) is shown.
- **NEG | I1** — No budget, project period, project partners, population covered, or urban area size for Brisbane; Brisbane is the conference venue and a few Brisbane suburbs appear as TC Alfred examples.
