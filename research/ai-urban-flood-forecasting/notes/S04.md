## I1 — Scope & scale

- **F01 | I1** — Sub-project title: demonstrator for AI-based operational 2D flood forecasting considering pump failure scenarios; funding programme "Anwender – Innovativ: Forschung für die zivile Sicherheit II", grant 13N16800, project period 01.12.2023–31.12.2025.
  Locator: S04 | PDF p.1 (unnumbered title page) | Title page
  Anchor: "Projektlaufzeit: 01.12.2023 – 31.12.2025"
  Context: Hydrotec sub-project. Compare S06 (Siegen sub-project): grant 13N16802, cover only "Auftrag vom: 10/2023" (S06 PDF p.1) — different sub-project grant numbers; S06 gives no end date.

- **F02 | I1** — Funder BMFTR, project management VDI Technologiezentrum; coordinator Emschergenossenschaft Lippeverband (EGLV), partner Universität Siegen FWU; Hydrotec project number P2929.
  Locator: S04 | PDF p.2 (printed p.1) | Impressum
  Anchor: "Bundesministerium für Forschung, Technologie und Raumfahrt (BMFTR)"
  Context: S06 names coordinator as "Lippeverband (LV)" (S06 PDF p.1); S04 lists "Emschergenossenschaft Lippeverband (EGLV)" as Verbundkoordinator but in text refers to "Projektpartnern Lippeverband (LV)" (PDF p.5).

- **F03 | I1** — Goal: AI-based real-time forecast of flood areas/depths for pumping stations and their polder areas, considering rainfall and discharge forecasts, current pump capacity and potential failure scenarios; to replace time-consuming hydraulic computations and provide event-related flood areas within a few minutes; to integrate into EGLV's operational Delft-FEWS system, thereby extending it to a pumping station on a tributary (Nebengewässer).
  Locator: S04 | PDF p.4 (printed p.3) | §1 "Zielsetzung und Stand der Technik"
  Anchor: "auf ein Pumpwerk an einem Nebengewässer aus-geweitet"
  Context: Focus on protecting people around pumping stations in mining subsidence areas.

- **F04 | I1** — Pilot area: pumping station Dorsten-Hammbach and the surrounding polder area.
  Locator: S04 | PDF p.4 (printed p.3) | §1
  Anchor: "Das Pumpwerk Dorsten-Hammbach und das umgebende Poldergebiet dienten als Pilotgebiet"
  Context: Pilot / demonstrator.

- **F05 | I1** — Six work packages (AP1 N-A scenarios; AP2 pump failure scenarios; AP3 KI training for 2D flood forecast; AP4 Delft-FEWS integration; AP5 disaster preparedness with end-user requirements; AP6 coordination & dissemination). ## I3 — AI forecasting model

- **F13 | I3** — KNN module (developed by FWU) inputs: accumulated rainfall and discharge of the last 24 h, forecast discharge and rainfall of the next 24 h, and the currently maximum possible pumping capacity of the pumping station.
  Locator: S04 | PDF p.6 (printed p.5) | §2.3 "Überflutungsvorhersage inkl. Pumpenausfallszenarien (Arbeitspaket Nr. 3)"
  Anchor: "die akkumulierten Niederschläge und Abflüsse der letzten 24 Stunden"
  Context: No architecture details in S04 (see S06 for ResNet18 CNN).

- **F14 | I3** — Eight trained KNN (one per pump failure scenario); weights and final software architecture handed over by FWU to Hydrotec; all eight weight sets stored in the KNN module container; the appropriate trained AI is selected based on current pump operating state and maximum possible capacity.
  Locator: S04 | PDF p.8 (printed p.7) | §2.4
  Anchor: "alle acht Ge-wichte wurden im KNN-Modul-Container hinterlegt"
  Context: Consistent with S06 (8 scenarios).

- **F15 | I3** — Output: maps of expected maximum inundation of the next 24 h at 5 × 5 m resolution; three maps per forecast (favourable / median / unfavourable).
  Locator: S04 | PDF p.8 (printed p.7) | §2.4 | Fig 2-3
  Anchor: "die erwartete maximale Überschwemmung der nächsten 24 Stunden mit einer Auflösung von 5 x 5 Metern"
  Context: Fig 2-3 legend depth classes (read from screenshot): ≥20, ≥50, ≥100, ≥150, ≥200, ≥400 cm; label "Dorsten KNN: Testevent 2025-11-24 09:40". Consistent with S06 5 m resolution.

- **F16 | I3** — Upstream forecast chain: N-A model driven by DWD ensemble forecast ICON-D2-EPS, updated every 3 h, covering next 48 h; discharge computed for all 20 ensemble members; exceedance percentiles 10/25/33/50/67/75/90 % computed; 10 %, 50 %, 90 % percentile hydrographs feed the flood computation.
  Locator: S04 | PDF p.7 (printed p.6) | §2.4
  Anchor: "mit allen 20 Ensemble-Mitgliedern berechnet"
  Context: Ensemble/percentile approach not described in S06.

- **F17 | I3** — Per flood forecast, three KNN runs in parallel: favourable (90 % percentile — 90 % of members forecast at least this rainfall/discharge), median, unfavourable (10 % percentile).
  Locator: S04 | PDF p.7–8 (printed p.6–7) | §2.4
  Anchor: "Für jede Überflutungsvor-hersage werden drei Modelläufe des KNN-Moduls parallel gestartet"
  Context: —

- **F18 | I3** — Triggering: 2D flood forecast for next 24 h runs automatically once the discharge forecast at gauge Dorsten "Am Strandbad" exceeds a defined threshold, repeated every 6 h until it falls below; manual run possible anytime; "Modifier" allow overriding all KNN inputs (what-if: reduced pump capacity, higher discharges).
  Locator: S04 | PDF p.8 (printed p.7) | §2.4
  Anchor: "erfolgt alle 6 Stunden bis zur Unterschreitung dieses Grenzwertes"
  Context: Threshold value not stated in text; Fig 2-2 (PDF p.7) shows a dashed line "Start Überflutungsvorhersage" at ≈ 6 m³/s on the discharge panel (read from screenshot). S06 describes two thresholds (min. cumulated discharge of training + critical discharge; S06 PDF p.22, printed p.19).

## I7 — IT infrastructure

- **F24 | I7** — Interface software written in Python by Hydrotec: time-series export from Delft-FEWS as XML → NumPy arrays for the KNN module; KNN results (NumPy) converted by the adapter into CF-1.6-compliant netCDF read by Delft-FEWS.
  Locator: S04 | PDF p.6 (printed p.5) | §2.3
  Anchor: "in eine CF-1.6 Kompatible netCDF-Datei umgewandelt"
  Context: Operational inference infrastructure, not training.

- **F25 | I7** — Because the KNN module requires a specific Python version and libraries, the model adapter was moved to its own Docker container; the Docker image runs isolated on a separate Linux server, independent of the forecasting system's Python environment.
  Locator: S04 | PDF p.7 (printed p.6) | §2.4
  Anchor: "Das Docker‑Image des Modelladapters wird dabei isoliert auf einem sepa-raten Linux‑Server betrieben"
  Context: Inference/deployment; no hardware specs (CPU/GPU) of that server given.
