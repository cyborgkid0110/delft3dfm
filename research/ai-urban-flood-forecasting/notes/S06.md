## I3 — AI forecasting model

- **F12 | I3** — Architecture: Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) acting as surrogate for the hydrodynamic model; based on ResNet18 (He et al. 2015), extended by a feature-informed training approach (Schmid & Leandro 2023); residual blocks with skip connections y = F(x,{Wᵢ}) + x.
  Locator: S06 | PDF p.10 (printed p.7) | §3.2.3 "Datengetriebene Vorhersage mittels Echtzeit-Aufzeichnung" | Fig 3-1
  Anchor: "Die Netzwerkarchitektur basiert auf einem ResNet18"
  Context: Fig 3-1 caption: "ResNet mit geospezifischem Trainingsansatz"; figure shows conv block, max pool, residual blocks (conv – Bn+relu – conv – Bn+relu), flatten, "Integration von Geoinformation", output "Wassertiefen".

- **F13 | I3** — Two parallel processing paths (backbones) for rainfall and discharge; inputs min–max normalised to [0,1] and converted into 17×17 images encoding the 24-h forecast horizon in time; tensor shapes (n, 17, 17, 2) for rainfall and (n, 17, 17, 4) for discharge. Outputs of both backbones are added element-wise and passed to class-specific layers with 256, 512 and n neurons, n = number of output cells per class.
  Locator: S06 | PDF p.10 (printed p.7) | §3.2.3
  Anchor: "der Dimension (n, 17, 17, 2) für Niederschlag und (n, 17, 17, 4) für Abfluss"
  Context: Output classes by height relative to the pumping station: class 1 < 7 m, class 2 7–11 m, class 3 > 11 m, class 4 = standing water (e.g. lake).

- **F14 | I3** — Output: spatially-resolved maximum flood depth per cell within the forecast window, for all failure scenarios, "within seconds". Training target = maximum water depth of each cell within the respective forecast window.
  Locator: S06 | PDF p.10 (printed p.7) §3.2.3; PDF p.11 (printed p.8) §3.2.3
  Anchor: "Als Zielgröße für das Training dient die maximale Wassertiefe jeder Zelle"
  Context: Output grid resolution follows the 5 m hydraulic model (F10) — the document does not explicitly state the AI output resolution separately.

- **F15 | I3** — Lead time: 24-h forecast horizon, set in agreement with local emergency services; dynamic approach: forecast limited to next 24 h, continued with a time offset of 6 h (rolling windows) for multi-day events.
  Locator: S06 | PDF p.11 (printed p.8) | §3.2.3 | Fig 3-2
  Anchor: "ein Vorhersagezeitraum von 24 Stunden festge-setzt"
  Context: Fig 3-2 shows "Zeitschritt 6 Std", windows t₀, t₁ … tₙ.

- **F16 | I3** — Inputs per path: current forecast window (rainfall/discharge of next 24 h) plus cumulated values of the previous window (last 24 h) as separate channels; the cumulated values act as implicit temporal memory to avoid error accumulation typical of autoregressive models.
  Locator: S06 | PDF p.11 (printed p.8) | §3.2.3 | Fig 3-3
  Anchor: "Die kumulierten Werte fungieren als implizites temporales Gedächtnis"
  Context: Fig 3-3 labels "Akkumulierter Abfluss/Regen der letzten 24 h" and "Abfluss und Regen der nächsten 24 h"; shows 3 hydrographs. Operational forecast uses rainfall & discharge forecasts of next 24 h and accumulated discharges of last 24 h (PDF p.22, printed p.19).

- **F17 | I3** — One separate ANN per pump failure scenario (8 scenarios → 8 models); the scenario-specific model is selected in operation.
  Locator: S06 | PDF p.6 (printed p.3) §2; PDF p.12 (printed p.9) §3.3
  Anchor: "wählt das entsprechende datengetriebene Modell für die Überflutungsvorhersage aus"
  Context: Number "8" derives from Table 2 (F19).

- **F18 | I3** — Complementary probabilistic component (AP2): Bayesian network (DAG) computing failure probability per pump from root nodes (a-priori, set manually), dependent variables (conditional probability tables) and end nodes (failure scenarios); updated via Bayes' theorem with new operating-state information; aggregated to station failure probability via chain rule. External mechanisms: power failure, pump-station flooding, sediment input; internal: material failure (abrasion, fatigue), failure of electronic control/sensor components.
  Locator: S06 | PDF p.7–8 (printed p.4–5) §3.1.2; PDF p.14–15 (printed p.11–12) §4.1.1 | Fig 4-1
  Anchor: "in ein Bayesisches Netz (BN) überführt, das als gerichteter azyklischer Graph"
  Context: Not a neural network; used to select the failure scenario in variant 2 (F32).

- **F19 | I3** — Failure scenarios: FS = 2ⁿ − 1 → 15 possible failure combinations for 4 pumps; merging capacity-equivalent combinations and including regular operation yields 8 significant scenarios with remaining capacity 15.5 / 13.5 / 11 / 9 / 6.5 / 4.5 / 2 / 0 m³/s (scenarios 1–8).
  Locator: S06 | PDF p.8 (printed p.5) §3.1.3; PDF p.15 (printed p.12) §4.1.2 | Table 2
  Anchor: "Mit Betrachtung des regulären Betriebs ergeben sich somit 8 signifikante Szenarien"
  Context: Scenario 1 = all pumps running; scenario 8 = total failure (0 m³/s).

## I6 — Results / outcomes

- **F31 | I6** — Integration into Delft-FEWS: two variants. Variant 1 (integrated into the existing Delft-FEWS system) derives the failure scenario from real-time on/off status of each pump; variant 2 computes failure probabilities via the Bayesian network from current input parameters and selects the corresponding model. Both implemented in Python; interface via NumPy arrays (input/output) and JSON files (pump status, BN parameters); automated data transfer after each forecast.
  Locator: S06 | PDF p.12 (printed p.9) §3.3; PDF p.21–22 (printed p.18–19) §4.3 | Fig 4-9
  Anchor: "Die erste Variante wurde in das bestehende Delft-FEWS-System integriert"
  Context: Text explicitly says variant 1 was integrated in the existing system; integration status of variant 2 not stated explicitly. Fig 4-9 flow: Niederschlagsvorhersage → N-A-Modell → Schwellenwert erreicht? → Datengetriebenes Modell → Überflutungsvorhersage in Echtzeit.

- **F32 | I6** — Operational triggering: two thresholds — (1) minimum cumulated discharge used for the first forecast window in training (ensures initial forecast lies within training data range), (2) a critical discharge associated with likely flood development; when both reached, a forecast is made from current pump records, rainfall/discharge forecasts of next 24 h and accumulated discharge of last 24 h.
  Locator: S06 | PDF p.22 (printed p.19) | §4.3
  Anchor: "wird ein Schwellenwert für die Initialisierung des Vorhersagesystems definiert"
  Context: Threshold values not given.

- **F33 | I6** — Speed: CNN gives real-time forecasts of max flood depths for all failure scenarios within seconds; computation time "only seconds to at most a few minutes"; inference on CPU is sufficient for operation.
  Locator: S06 | PDF p.10 (printed p.7) §3.2.3; PDF p.13 (printed p.10) §3.3; PDF p.24 (printed p.21) §5
  Anchor: "schnelle Berechnungszeit von nur Sekunden bis maximal wenigen Minuten"
  Context: No runtime of the 1D/2D model given for a speed-up factor.

- **F34 | I6** — Qualitative results: pluvial and fluvial flooding around the discharge peak reliably captured (Fig 4-6, scenario 5 at 90 h); only the lake shows higher deviation (no influence on urban flood development). Higher errors in the recession phase (scenario 5 at 126 h, Fig 4-7) due to increasing accumulation values with decreasing discharge; larger deviations in rarely flooded areas under low pumping capacity/station failure (scenario 8 at 78 h, Fig 4-8) because extreme events are under-represented in the data; higher RMSE considered small relative to local depths of partly > 4 m. Conclusion: "very good" forecasts of max depths for the decisive phases, accurate for operational service.
  Locator: S06 | PDF p.19–21 (printed p.16–18) | §4.2.2 | Figs 4-6, 4-7, 4-8
  Anchor: "Solche Extremereignisse sind aufgrund ihrer sel-tenen Häufigkeit in Daten unterrepräsentiert"
  Context: Assessment relative to hydrodynamic simulations, not to observed real floods.
