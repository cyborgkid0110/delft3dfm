## I3 — AI forecasting model

- **F16 | I3** — **Overall aim of KIWaSuS:** a real-time warning and real-time management system for urban flash floods using AI methods, "insbesondere maschinelle Lernverfahren" (especially machine learning). It should significantly increase warning lead times, localise floods better, and supply information to municipal crisis management.
  Locator: S07 | PDF p.7 (printed p.4) | §2.1; also PDF p.2 (printed p.2) Teil I §1
  Anchor: "Einsatz von KI-Verfahren, insbesondere maschinellen Lernverfahren"
  Context: The specific ML methods or architectures (e.g. network type) are **not** named anywhere in S07.

- **F17 | I3** — **System chain (Fig. 3 "Gesamtsystem des Forschungsprojektes"):**
  - **Data acquisition:** precipitation from radar measurements; precipitation from ground measurement; flow classes ("Durchflussklassen") measured in the sewer.
  - **Data processing:** plausibility check and correction ("Plausibilisierung/Korrektur (Datenverarbeitung)").
  - **Forecast process:** real-time correction of radar data ("Echtzeitkorrektur (Datenverarbeitung)") → precipitation forecast model → surcharge forecast model ("Überstau (Prognosemodell)") → flood-area forecast model ("Überflutungsflächen (Prognosemodell)").
  - **Delivery:** dashboard visualisation and WMS raster.
  - **Use:** warning levels and measures for expert users; information for other interested parties.

- **F27 | I6** — **Delivered system components:**

  - WMS services for rescue services and other interested parties.

  Locator: S07 | PDF p.14–15 (printed p.11–12) | §3.3.1.2 | Fig. 4 "Benutzeroberflächen für unterschiedliche Benutzergruppen"; PDF p.17 (printed p.14)

  Context: Logos and labels were read from the figure. Teil I says the specialist application "erfolgreich erprobt werden konnte" (was successfully tested).

- **F28 | I6** — **Warning and action concept**, modelled on the river-flood concept HOWIS, which uses a 6-level matrix:
  - **Pre-warning:** based on the DWD severe-weather warning. Gully checks at known hotspots, clearing of watercourse inlet racks, check whether to increase standby crews.
  - **Warning level 1:** flooding expected, ~60 min before rain. Gully checks at forecast hotspots; inform the fire brigade control centre.
  - **Warning level 2:** at least one forecast hotspot exceeds **25 cm** water depth, the depth at which vehicles may no longer pass. Targeted response; support the fire brigade.
  - **Warning level 3:** rain forecast gives no all-clear, flooding is occurring or increasing. Treated as a major event; the drainage operator supports the fire brigade; a crisis staff and the fire brigade "Einsatzkonzept Unwetter" may be activated.
  Locator: S07 | PDF p.19–21 (printed p.16–18) | §3.3.2 | Fig. 7 "Warn- und Handlungskonzept"
  Anchor: "überschreitet mindestens ein prognostizierter Überflutungsschwerpunkt eine Wassertiefe von 25 cm"
  Context: Hotspot information should be passed to the fire brigade preferably by telephone, because the control centre is flooded with messages during storms.

- **F29 | I6** — **Limits of operational use found:**
  - Only the fire brigade is directly involved in defence against flash floods, and it cannot take precautionary measures; it only responds to emergency calls.
  - Other city departments (Referat 69 traffic, Referat 32 public order) cannot carry out precautionary road closures. Barriers are permits, logistics, staff, material, working hours and missing standby duty.
  - The ~60 min lead time is too short for precautionary action. The fire brigade would need **at least 6 hours** for evacuations.
  - Integration with traffic-signal control was only discussed, not tested.
  Locator: S07 | PDF p.18–19 (printed p.15–16) | §3.3.1.3
  Anchor: "mindestens 6 Stunden Vorlaufzeit benötigen"
  Context: The original goal of involving more city departments could not be achieved within the project.

- **F30 | I6** — **Practical test outcome:** delivery as a WMS service and integration into existing systems was judged sensible and useful. Feedback went back to AP4. Simulated events were tested with sewer operations and the fire brigade (see F08, F22).
  Locator: S07 | PDF p.21 (printed p.18) | §3.3.3
  Anchor: "Die Umsetzung als WMS-Dienst und die Einbindung in bestehende Systeme wurde als sinnvoll"

- **F31 | I6** — **Expected benefits and next steps:**
  - more targeted deployment of operating staff
  - stopping sewer work in forecast areas at warning level 1
  - a permanent water-level gauge planned by AGG in the project-area underpass

  - citizen warnings via local radio, city social media or the "GE-meldet" app
  - integrating low-cost sensors and AI data-quality management (AP3) into the city environmental sensor network ("Vernetzte Stadt")
